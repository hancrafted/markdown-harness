# Tomorrow's weather

Rain arrives before dawn and stays through the morning. The wind backs south-westerly and
strengthens to around forty kilometres an hour by midday, easing again after dark.

Highs of nine degrees, lows of four. Bring a coat with a hood; an umbrella will be a liability in
that wind.

There is a clear spell in the middle of the afternoon, long enough for the pavements to dry, before
a second band of rain moves in overnight. Ferries on the western crossings are likely to be
cancelled from mid-morning.
