Han is the author of `markdown-harness` and, in its own vocabulary, its only Operator so far — he
writes the config, owns the vision docs, and is the person who will run the workshops the product
is aimed at.

He works by **grilling**: he wants premises attacked, not confirmed. Recommendations must be
argued from evidence in the repo (a measured number, a quoted tenet, a line in a research file),
and he will say so when reasoning is thin. He is comfortable being contradicted — in the vision
session he recorded withdrawn ideas with reasons so they would not be reinvented, and issue #1
carries a `⚠️ contradiction on record` entry rather than a silent fix.

He also reasons out loud mid-answer and will say when he is unsure ("you can see here I'm still
striving myself with the reasoning"). Treat that as an invitation to sharpen the framing, not as a
settled answer. See [[grilling-format-prose-not-picker]].
