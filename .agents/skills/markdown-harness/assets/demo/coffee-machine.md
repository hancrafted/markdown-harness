# Descaling the office coffee machine

The machine in the third-floor kitchen wants a descaling cycle roughly every two hundred cups, which
in practice means whenever the light on the front panel turns amber.

1. Empty the drip tray and the used-puck drawer, and take the water tank out.
2. Fill the tank to the MAX line with the solution from the cupboard above the sink, then top it up
   with cold water.
3. Put the tank back, hold the two outer buttons together until the amber light blinks, and let the
   cycle run. It takes about twenty minutes and it is loud.
4. Run two full tanks of plain water through afterwards. The solution tastes of almost nothing on
   its own, but it ruins the first three coffees if you skip this.

If the amber light comes back on within a week of a cycle, the tank filter is saturated rather than
the boiler. Spare filters are in the same cupboard.
